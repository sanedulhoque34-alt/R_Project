About Us

SHAH LIVING LLP – The Force Behind SECUREX

SHAH LIVING LLP is the proud manufacturer of the SECUREX brand of premium steel furniture. Under the leadership of Brajesh Steel, we specialize in producing high-quality steel almirahs, storage cabinets, and customized metal furniture for B2B clients across Odisha and beyond.

Our strength lies in advanced manufacturing with Fiber Laser Cutting Machines, CNC Bending Machines, Almirah Printing Machines, and Glossy Finishing Units — delivering superior accuracy and premium finish in every product.

With a focus on bulk production, customization, and timely delivery, we are the trusted partners for wholesalers, retailers, and institutional buyers.
